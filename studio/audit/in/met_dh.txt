# meteorology-b / dh: Drought and heat waves  (37 questions, 21 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Drought: long stretch with too little precipitation. Heat wave: long stretch of abnormal heat. Both are often caused by a blocking high-pressure ridge that sits in place and steers storms away. Effects: crop loss, water limits, wildfire, heat illness, power demand.
