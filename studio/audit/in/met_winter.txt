## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Storm Key facts Blizzard Wind or gusts ≥ 35 mph + falling/blowing snow + visibility under ¼ mile, all for 3+ hours Nor'easter Strong East Coast storm; northeast winds; snow, coastal flooding, wind Lake effect Cold air over warm open lake water, narrow heavy snow bands downwind Ice storm Freezing rain builds up ice. ¼ inch can bring down lines A blizzard does not need new snow. Wind can blow old snow.
