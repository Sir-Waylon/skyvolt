# meteorology-b / winds: Damaging winds  (29 questions, 24 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Straight-line winds = damage not from rotation. Damage is spread out in one direction. Term Meaning Downdraft Sinking air Downburst Downdraft that hits the ground and spreads out Microburst Downburst under 4 km wide. Brief, intense Macroburst Downburst 4 km or wider. Longer lasting Gust front Leading edge of cool outflow Squall line Organized line of storms Derecho Long-lived, widespread windstorm. Track of hundreds of miles Downslope wind Air accelerating down a mountain slope On radar: bow echo (derecho/damaging wind), fine line (gust front), bowing segment on a squall line.
