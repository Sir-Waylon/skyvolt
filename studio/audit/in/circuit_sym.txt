# circuit-lab-b / sym: Symbolic logic  (155 questions, 40 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Connectives: ∧ and, ∨ or, → if…then, ~ not, ↔ if and only if. The antecedent is the "if" part. Find the main connective by commas and "both…and", "either…or", "if…then". Rule Form Modus ponens P→Q, P ∴ Q Modus tollens P→Q, ~Q ∴ ~P Hypothetical syllogism P→Q, Q→R ∴ P→R Disjunctive syllogism P∨Q, ~P ∴ Q Conjunctive elimination / addition P∧Q ∴ P; P, Q ∴ P∧Q Disjunctive addition P ∴ P∨Q Constructive dilemma P→Q, R→S, P∨R ∴ Q∨S Double negation, tautology, commutation ~~P = P; P∨P = P; P∨Q = Q∨P Equivalence P↔Q = (P→Q)∧(Q→P) De Morgan ~(P∧Q) = ~P∨~Q; ~(P∨Q) = ~P∧~Q Trap Affirming the consequent (Q, so P) and denying the antecedent (~P, so ~Q) are invalid. In a proof, P in the reason column means premise.
