## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Ice and graupel collide in the cloud. Smaller, lighter ice rises with positive charge. Heavier graupel falls with negative charge. The cloud base is mostly negative. The ground below turns positive. Type Path Cloud-to-ground Cloud to the surface Intracloud Inside one cloud Cloud-to-cloud Between two clouds Cloud-to-air Cloud into clear air Lightning detection systems locate strikes using sensors across a network (they time the signal at each sensor). Miles = seconds between flash and thunder ÷ 5
