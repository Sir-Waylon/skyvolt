# meteorology-b / maps: Maps, station models, METAR  (74 questions, 62 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
A station model packs temperature, dew point, pressure, wind and sky cover into one symbol. Spot Means Upper left Temperature Lower left Dew point Upper right Pressure, last 3 digits (tenths of mb) Lower right 3-hour pressure change Center circle Sky cover (more filled = cloudier) Barb staff Points toward where wind COMES FROM Half flag / full / pennant 5 kt / 10 kt / 50 kt Pressure: 500 or more → put 9 in front. Less than 500 → put 10. 132 → 1013.2 mb 987 → 998.7 mb 047 → 1004.7 mb METAR example: KOKC 202153Z 18018G28KT 6SM TSRA BKN040CB 27/22 A2985 Part Meaning KOKC Station 202153Z Day 20, 21:53 UTC 18018G28KT From 180° at 18 kt, gusts 28 kt 6SM Visibility 6 statute miles TSRA Thunderstorm with rain BKN040CB Broken clouds at 4,000 ft, cumulonimbus 27/22 Temp 27°C, dew point 22°C A2985 Altimeter 29.85 inHg Sky: FEW 1–2/8, SCT 3–4/8, BKN 5–7/8, OVC 8/8. Height × 100 ft. Negative temps start with M (M05 = −5°C).
