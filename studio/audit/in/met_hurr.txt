# meteorology-b / hurr: Hurricanes  (48 questions, 35 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Hurricane = typhoon = cyclone (same storm, different ocean). Needs warm water (about 26.5 °C or more), moisture, low shear, and distance from the equator. Life cycle: Invest → Depression (under 39 mph) → Tropical Storm (39–73) → Hurricane (74+) → Major (Cat 3+). Saffir-Simpson Sustained wind Cat 1 74–95 mph Cat 2 96–110 mph Cat 3 111–129 mph (major) Cat 4 130–156 mph (major) Cat 5 157+ mph (major) Parts: eye (calm, 20–40 mi), eyewall (worst winds and rain), spiral bands (can spawn tornadoes). Storm surge = water pushed onshore. Biggest with strong wind, large size, slow movement, and shallow, funnel-shaped coasts. Often the deadliest hazard. Land cuts off the heat source, so storms weaken. Shear and dry air weaken them. El Niño tends to raise shear in the Atlantic and quiet it. Atlantic season: June 1 – Nov 30.
