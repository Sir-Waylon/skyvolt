# circuit-lab-b / hist: History  (44 questions, 44 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Who Year Know this Coulomb 1785 Inverse-square force between charges (torsion balance); unit of charge Volta 1800 Voltaic pile (zinc and copper discs with brine-soaked cloth): first chemical battery; the volt Ampère 1820s Founded electrodynamics; parallel currents attract or repel; link between electricity and magnetism; the ampere Ohm 1827 V = IR; the ohm Kirchhoff 1845 Current law (charge conservation) and voltage law (energy conservation) Boole 1854 Algebra of logic on two values (0 and 1); the basis of logic gates Hertz 1887 Generated and detected electromagnetic (radio) waves; the hertz = 1 cycle per second Ørsted 1820 Current deflects a compass: first link of electricity and magnetism Faraday 1831 Electromagnetic induction; the farad Tesla 1880s AC induction motor, polyphase AC; the tesla (flux density) The slides also name Thales (amber), Franklin (naming + and −, conventional current), Tesla (AC). The "Big Three" are Volta, Ampère, Ohm.
