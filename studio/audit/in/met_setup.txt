## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Recipe: moisture + instability + lift + wind shear. Ingredient Role Jet stream Steers storms, adds energy aloft Cold front Cold air pushes in. Fast, sharp storms Warm front Warm air slides over cold. Wide, steady precipitation Stationary Stalled boundary Occluded Cold front catches warm front Dryline Moisture boundary (moist east, dry west on the Plains). Not a temperature boundary Atmospheric river Narrow stream of heavy water vapor. Extreme rain at landfall Wind shear Wind changes with height. Organizes storms; directional shear starts rotation Fetch Distance wind blows over water Ocean heat content Warm deep water fuels hurricanes. Storm wakes are cooler Mountains lift air (trigger storms), and can block, weaken or redirect storms.
