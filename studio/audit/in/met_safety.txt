# meteorology-b / safety: Hazards and safety  (29 questions, 28 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Storm Main hazard Tornado Violent wind, debris Hurricane Storm surge, wind, inland flooding Severe thunderstorm Hail, wind, lightning Flash flood Rapid water rise Lightning Direct strike, fires Winter storm Snow/ice load, low visibility, cold Drought/heat Water shortage, heat illness Alert Means Watch Conditions favorable. Get ready Warning Happening or about to. Act now Advisory Less serious but still disruptive Actions: tornado = lowest floor, interior room, no windows. Hurricane = follow evacuation orders. Flash flood = go high, never drive through water. Lightning = indoors or hard-topped car. Generators outside only (carbon monoxide). Reliable sources: weather.gov, spc.noaa.gov (storms), nhc.noaa.gov (tropics).
