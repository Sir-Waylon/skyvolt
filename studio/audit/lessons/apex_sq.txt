## LESSON sq-L3: Sigma notation · Recursive and explicit rules
KEY sq-L3#0 | Sigma notation | Σ means add. Σ from k = 1 to 4 of 2k means 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20.
KEY sq-L3#1 | Recursive and explicit rules | Recursive: each term from the one before (aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + 3). Explicit: each term from its position (aₙ = 3n + 1).
