## LESSON sr-L1: Sequences and partial sums · Convergence tests
KEY sr-L1#0 | Sequences and partial sums | A series adds the terms of a sequence. It converges if its partial sums settle toward a number. A geometric series a + ar + ar² + … converges to a/(1 − r) when |r| < 1. | FORMULA: S = a/(1 − r), |r| < 1 | CHECK Q: Find the sum of the geometric series 2 + 3/2 + 9/8 + …
KEY sr-L1#1 | Convergence tests | If the terms do not go to 0, the series diverges. A p-series Σ1/nᵖ converges exactly when p > 1. The ratio test: if |aₙ₊₁/aₙ| → L, converge when L < 1, diverge when L > 1. | FORMULA: Σ1/nᵖ converges ⇔ p > 1 | CHECK Q: For aₙ = 2/3ⁿ, the ratio aₙ₊₁/aₙ tends to 2/3. Does Σaₙ converge? Type 1 for yes, 0 for no.
KEY sr-L1#2 | Taylor series | A smooth function near 0 can be matched by a polynomial: f(x) ≈ f(0) + f′(0)x + f″(0)x²/2! + …. For eˣ every derivative at 0 is 1, so eˣ = 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/3! + …. | FORMULA: eˣ = Σ xⁿ/n! | CHECK Q: In the Taylor series eˣ = 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/3! + …, what is the coefficient of x^4? (Decimal, 4 places)
KEY sr-L1#3 | Parametric curves | x = x(t), y = y(t) trace a path. The slope is dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt), and the speed is √((dx/dt)² + (dy/dt)²). | FORMULA: dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt)
