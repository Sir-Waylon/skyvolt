## LESSON lin-L1: Slope · Slope-intercept form
KEY lin-L1#0 | Slope | Slope tells how steep a line is: rise over run. Up-right is positive; down-right is negative. | FORMULA: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁) | CHECK Q: A zip line passes through the grid points (−1, 5) and (−6, −3). Find its slope. Fractions are fine.
KEY lin-L1#1 | Slope-intercept form | In y = mx + b, m is the slope and b is where the line crosses the y-axis. | FORMULA: y = 2x − 3 | CHECK Q: A line with slope 4 passes through (2, −4). What is its y-intercept?
KEY lin-L1#2 | Make a line from a point | Use the slope and a point to find b: plug x and y into y = mx + b, then solve for b. | CHECK Q: A zip line passes through the grid points (6, −2) and (8, −2). Find its slope. Fractions are fine.
