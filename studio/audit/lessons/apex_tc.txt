## LESSON tc-L1: Turns and degrees · The sides of a right triangle
KEY tc-L1#0 | Turns and degrees | A full turn is 360°. Half a turn is 180°, a quarter turn is 90°. The three angles of any triangle always add to 180°.
KEY tc-L1#1 | The sides of a right triangle | In a right triangle the longest side, across from the right angle, is the hypotenuse. For an angle you pick, the other two sides are the opposite and the adjacent. | FORMULA: a² + b² = c² | CHECK Q: A right triangle has opposite side 5, adjacent side 12 and hypotenuse 13 for angle A. What is sin A (as a decimal)?
KEY tc-L1#2 | Sine, cosine and tangent | SOH-CAH-TOA: sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tan = opposite ÷ adjacent. They are ratios, so they do not depend on how big the triangle is. | FORMULA: sin θ = opp/hyp | CHECK Q: A right triangle has opposite side 5, adjacent side 12 and hypotenuse 13 for angle A. What is cos A (as a decimal)?
## LESSON tc-L2: Finding sides and angles · Radians
KEY tc-L2#0 | Finding sides and angles | To find a side, write the ratio that has it and solve. To find an angle, use the inverse button: sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ or tan⁻¹. | CHECK Q: A right triangle has opposite side 6, adjacent side 8 and hypotenuse 10 for angle A. What is cos A (as a decimal)?
KEY tc-L2#1 | Radians | One radian is the angle that cuts off an arc as long as the radius. A half-turn is π radians, so 180° = π rad. | FORMULA: degrees × π/180 = radians | CHECK Q: A clock face has radius 9 cm. The tip of the minute hand sweeps through 180°. How long is the arc it traces (cm, 2 decimals)?
KEY tc-L2#2 | The unit circle | On a circle of radius 1, the point at angle θ is (cos θ, sin θ). So cosine is the x-coordinate and sine is the y-coordinate. | CHECK Q: A clock face has radius 9 cm. The tip of the minute hand sweeps through 60°. How long is the arc it traces (cm, 2 decimals)?
