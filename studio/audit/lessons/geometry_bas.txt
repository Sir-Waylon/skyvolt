## LESSON bas-L2: Angle pairs · Vertical angles
KEY bas-L2#0 | Angle pairs | Complementary add to 90°. Supplementary add to 180°. A linear pair is two adjacent angles that make a straight line. | CHECK Q: Proof: Vertical angles are equal. What is the reason for “m∠1 + m∠2 = 180°”?
KEY bas-L2#1 | Vertical angles | When two lines cross, the angles directly across from each other are equal. | FORMULA: ∠1 = ∠3 | CHECK Q: Two sidewalks cross in an X. One angle between them is 62°. What is the angle directly opposite it (degrees)?
KEY bas-L2#2 | Segments and midpoints | A midpoint splits a segment into two equal parts. A bisector splits a segment or angle in half. | FORMULA: AM = MB | CHECK Q: Two sidewalks cross in an X. One angle between them is 61°. What is the angle directly opposite it (degrees)?
KEY bas-L2#3 | Properties of equality | Reflexive: a = a. Symmetric: if a = b then b = a. Transitive: if a = b and b = c then a = c. Substitution lets you swap equals.
