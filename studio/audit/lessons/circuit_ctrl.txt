## LESSON ctrl-L3: Relay · Switch logic
KEY ctrl-L3#0 | Relay | A small current in a coil closes a switch in a different circuit. Lets a tiny signal control big power. A diode across the coil absorbs the spike. | CHECK Q: Which device is described? "Uses a small coil current to switch a separate circuit"
KEY ctrl-L3#1 | Switch logic | Switches in series = AND (both must be on). Switches in parallel = OR (either one works). | CHECK Q: A lamp should light only when a door switch AND a key switch are both closed. How should they be wired?
