## LESSON flu-L2: Buoyancy · Continuity
KEY flu-L2#0 | Buoyancy | A fluid pushes up with a force equal to the weight of the fluid the object pushes aside (Archimedes). F_B = ρ_fluid V_displaced g. | FORMULA: F_B = ρVg | CHECK Q: A fully submerged object has volume 4 litres (0.004 m³). What buoyant force does fresh water (1000 kg/m³) exert on it? Use g = 10 m/s².
