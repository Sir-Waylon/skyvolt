## LESSON winter-L1: Blizzard · No new snow needed
KEY winter-L1#0 | Blizzard | Wind or gusts of 35 mph or more, snow in the air, and visibility under ¼ mile. All for at least 3 hours. | CHECK Q: A blizzard needs sustained winds or frequent gusts of at least how many mph?

## LESSON winter-L2: Lake effect · Ice storm
KEY winter-L2#0 | Lake effect | Cold air over warm open lake water makes narrow heavy snow bands downwind. | CHECK Q: A lake-effect snow forms when cold air moves across what?
KEY winter-L2#1 | Ice storm | Freezing rain coats everything. ¼ inch can break lines and branches. | CHECK Q: Which wintry precipitation builds up the heaviest ice on power lines: sleet or freezing rain?
