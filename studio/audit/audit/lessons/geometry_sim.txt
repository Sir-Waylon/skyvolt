## LESSON sim-L1: Ratio and proportion · Similar figures
KEY sim-L1#0 | Ratio and proportion | A ratio compares two numbers. A proportion says two ratios are equal. Cross-multiply to solve. | FORMULA: a/b = c/d → ad = bc | CHECK Q: A poster and its small copy have matching lengths in the ratio 7 : 6. The poster length is 21 cm. Find the copy’s matching length (cm).
KEY sim-L1#1 | Similar figures | Same shape, different size. Corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are in the same ratio (the scale factor). | FORMULA: △ABC ∼ △DEF | CHECK Q: Which is enough to prove two triangles similar?
KEY sim-L1#2 | Prove triangles similar | AA is enough. Also SAS∼ (two sides in proportion, included angle equal) and SSS∼ (all three sides in proportion).
