## LESSON logic-L1: Digital basics · Counting rows
KEY logic-L1#0 | Digital basics | Everything is 1 (on, true) or 0 (off, false). A truth table lists every input and its output. | CHECK Q: How many input rows does the truth table of a 4-input gate have?
KEY logic-L1#1 | Counting rows | n inputs give 2ⁿ rows. Three inputs = 8 rows. | CHECK Q: For how many of the 8 input rows is the Carry-out of a full adder equal to 1?
KEY logic-L1#2 | AND, OR, NOT | AND = 1 only if both are 1. OR = 1 if either is 1. NOT flips it. | CHECK Q: Name the 2-input gate whose output is 0 only when both inputs are 1.
## LESSON logic-L2: NAND, NOR · XOR, XNOR
KEY logic-L2#0 | NAND, NOR | NAND is AND then flipped. NOR is OR then flipped. They are "universal": any circuit can be built from just one of them.
KEY logic-L2#1 | XOR, XNOR | XOR = 1 when inputs are different. XNOR = 1 when inputs match. | CHECK Q: Which gate outputs 1 when its two inputs are equal?
KEY logic-L2#2 | Precedence | Do NOT first, then AND, then OR. Like multiply before add. | CHECK Q: By operator precedence, NOT is applied first, then AND, then OR. For A = 0, B = 1, C = 0, the value of A + B·C is:

## LESSON logic-L4: NAND-only builds · Multiplexer
KEY logic-L4#0 | NAND-only builds | NOT = 1 NAND (tie inputs). AND = 2. OR = 3. XOR = 4. | CHECK Q: Name the 2-input gate whose output is 1 only when its inputs differ.
KEY logic-L4#1 | Multiplexer | Picks one input using select lines. 2ⁿ inputs need n select lines. 8 inputs need 3. | CHECK Q: How many select lines does a 4:1 multiplexer need? (Round up to a whole number.)
KEY logic-L4#2 | Half adder | Adds two bits. Sum = A XOR B. Carry = A AND B. | CHECK Q: A half adder receives A=0, B=0. Outputs?
## LESSON logic-L5: Full adder · Binary counting
KEY logic-L5#0 | Full adder | Adds two bits plus a carry. Sum is 1 when an odd number of inputs are 1. | CHECK Q: For how many of the 8 input combinations is the sum output of a full adder equal to 1?
KEY logic-L5#1 | Binary counting | Place values: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32. 101101 = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45. | CHECK Q: A binary number ends in 1. Is the number odd or even?
KEY logic-L5#2 | Binary addition | 0+0=0. 0+1=1. 1+1=10 (write 0, carry 1). 1+1+1=11. | FORMULA: 1101 + 1001 = 10110 | CHECK Q: 1001 + 1101 in binary = ?
KEY logic-L5#3 | How many bits? | n bits can hold 0 up to 2ⁿ − 1. 6 bits max out at 63. | CHECK Q: Adding two 4-bit numbers can produce a result that needs at most how many bits?
