## LESSON rot-L1: Angular motion · Torque
KEY rot-L1#0 | Angular motion | Rotation uses angle θ (radians), angular velocity ω = Δθ/Δt and angular acceleration α = Δω/Δt. The equations match straight-line motion: ω = ω₀ + αt and θ = ω₀t + ½αt². | FORMULA: ω = ω₀ + αt | CHECK Q: A net torque of 15 N·m acts on a wheel with moment of inertia 4 kg·m². What is its angular acceleration?
KEY rot-L1#1 | Torque | A force turns an object only if it acts at a distance from the pivot. Torque = r F sin θ, where θ is the angle between the lever arm r and the force. | FORMULA: τ = rF sin θ | CHECK Q: A 39 N force pushes on a wrench at a distance of 1.2 m from the bolt, at 45° to the wrench. What is the torque? (3 significant figures)
KEY rot-L1#2 | Moment of inertia | I measures how hard it is to change rotation. It grows with mass and with distance from the axis squared. Point masses: I = Σmr². Solid disk about its centre: ½MR². Hoop: MR². Rod about its centre: ML²/12. | FORMULA: I = Σmr² | CHECK Q: Two small masses, 4 kg at 1 m and 3 kg at 2 m, are fixed to a light rod spinning about an axis. What is the moment of inertia of the system?
## LESSON rot-L2: Newton's second law for rotation · Rotational kinetic energy
KEY rot-L2#0 | Newton's second law for rotation | Net torque = I α. A bigger moment of inertia means a smaller angular acceleration for the same torque. | FORMULA: τ_net = Iα | CHECK Q: A net torque of 29 N·m acts on a wheel with moment of inertia 2 kg·m². What is its angular acceleration?
KEY rot-L2#1 | Rotational kinetic energy | A spinning object stores energy ½Iω². A rolling object has both ½mv² and ½Iω². | FORMULA: KE = ½Iω² | CHECK Q: A solid disk of mass 2 kg and radius 0.3 m spins at 18 rad/s about its centre. What is its rotational kinetic energy? (I = ½MR²)
KEY rot-L2#2 | Angular momentum | L = Iω. With no net external torque L is conserved: a skater who pulls her arms in lowers I, so ω rises. | FORMULA: L = Iω | CHECK Q: A net torque of 16 N·m acts on a wheel with moment of inertia 4 kg·m². What is its angular acceleration?
KEY rot-L2#3 | Rolling without slipping | The contact point is at rest, so v = ωR. Rolling down a slope the energy splits between moving and spinning, so rolling objects accelerate more slowly than sliding ones. | FORMULA: v = ωR
