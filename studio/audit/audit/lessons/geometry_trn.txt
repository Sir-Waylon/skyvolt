## LESSON trn-L2: Rotation · Symmetry
KEY trn-L2#0 | Rotation | Turn about a point. About the origin: 90° → (−y, x), 180° → (−x, −y), 270° → (y, −x). | CHECK Q: A rotation of 360° about a point is the same as…
KEY trn-L2#1 | Symmetry | A figure has line symmetry if a flip maps it onto itself. It has rotational symmetry if a turn less than 360° maps it onto itself.
