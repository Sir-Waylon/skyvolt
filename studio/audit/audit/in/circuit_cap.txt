# circuit-lab-b / cap: Capacitance  (34 questions, 12 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Two conductors separated by an insulator. C = Q/V (farad; usually µF). Energy E = ½CV² (doubling V gives 4× the energy). Parallel plates: C grows with plate area and with a dielectric, shrinks as the gap grows. Parallel: C adds. Series: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ (opposite of resistors). Once charged in DC, it acts like an open circuit. Electrolytic capacitors are polarized: wrong polarity can fail. Example. 100 µF at 12 V: Q = 1.2 mC; E = 7.2 mJ.
