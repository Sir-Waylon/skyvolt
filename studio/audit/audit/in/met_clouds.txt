## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Cloud Signal Cumulonimbus The thunderstorm cloud Wall cloud Lowered, often rotating base under the updraft. Tornado warning sign Shelf cloud Wedge at the leading edge. Marks the gust front Funnel cloud Rotating cone not touching ground Mammatus Pouches under an anvil. Often a storm weakening Funnel cloud = not on the ground. Once it touches down it is a tornado.
