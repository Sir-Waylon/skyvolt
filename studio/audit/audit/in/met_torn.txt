# meteorology-b / torn: Tornadoes and radar  (38 questions, 28 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Tornado = violently rotating column touching the ground and a cloud base. Waterspout = same over water. Peak: late afternoon, spring, Plains and Southeast. EF scale 3-second gust EF0 65–85 mph EF1 86–110 mph EF2 111–135 mph EF3 136–165 mph EF4 166–200 mph EF5 over 200 mph F scale = before 1 Feb 2007. EF scale = on or after. Both rate DAMAGE, not measured wind. Signature Means Hook echo Rain/debris wrapping a mesocyclone (reflectivity) TVS Tight inbound-outbound velocity pair = intense rotation Debris ball Low CC inside the hook = debris in the air Bow echo Straight-line wind; may hide small tornadoes
