# circuit-lab-b / kirch: Kirchhoff  (51 questions, 16 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
KCL: current into a node = current out. (Charge is conserved.) KVL: the sum of voltage rises and drops around any closed loop = 0. (Energy is conserved.) Two-loop recipe (two unknowns at most) Label a current in each branch and pick directions. A wrong guess just gives a negative answer. Write KCL at one node. Write KVL around two independent loops. A resistor is a drop in the direction of current; a source is a rise when you go through it from − to +. Solve the pair of equations. Check that power supplied = power dissipated. Example. E₁ = 19 V and R₁ = 2 Ω on the left, E₂ = 17 V and R₂ = 4 Ω on the right, R₃ = 6 Ω shared. I₃ = I₁ + I₂; 19 = 2I₁ + 6I₃; 17 = 4I₂ + 6I₃. Substitute: 19 = 8I₁ + 6I₂ and 17 = 6I₁ + 10I₂, so I₁ = 2.0 A, I₂ = 0.5 A, I₃ = 2.5 A.
