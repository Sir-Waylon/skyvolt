# circuit-lab-b / ctrl: Control devices  (64 questions, 26 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Device What it does SPST switch One pole, one throw: simple on/off SPDT switch Common terminal connects to one of two others DPDT switch Two poles switched together; cross-wire it to reverse a motor Relay Small coil current closes contacts in a separate circuit (NO = open at rest, NC = closed at rest). Add a reverse diode across the coil. Fuse Metal element melts on overcurrent; replace it. In series with the load. Slow-blow tolerates brief surges (motors). Circuit breaker Trips on overcurrent (heat bends a bimetal strip; a coil handles big shorts); resettable GFCI Trips when hot and neutral currents differ by about 5 mA (leak to ground, e.g. through a person). Protects people. Fuse size: I = P/V, then choose the smallest standard rating at or above I. 90 W at 12 V = 7.5 A → 10 A. Breaker check: I = P/V. A 2400 W load on 120 V draws 20 A and trips a 15 A breaker. Switch logic: switches in series = AND (both closed to light). Switches in parallel = OR (either closed). Three-way circuit: two SPDT switches. The commons go to the source (switch 1) and the lamp (switch 2); two traveler wires join the other terminals. Flipping either switch toggles the lamp.
