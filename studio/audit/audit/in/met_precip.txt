# meteorology-b / precip: Precipitation and floods  (39 questions, 32 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
How it forms: Process Result Collision-coalescence Drops merge into bigger drops (warm clouds) Bergeron process Ice crystals grow while supercooled drops shrink (cold clouds) Riming Supercooled drops freeze onto ice (builds graupel and hail) Aggregation Ice crystals stick together (snowflakes) Type Layers it fell through Snow Cold all the way down Sleet Snow melts, then refreezes as ice pellets in a cold layer near the ground Freezing rain Rain falls on a surface below 0 °C and freezes on contact Hail Strong updraft keeps stones aloft, they grow by riming Rain Warm all the way down Flood types: flash (minutes to hours), stream/river (rises over days), urban (drains overwhelmed). Burn scars and steep land produce debris flows and mudslides. Hail: pea ¼ in, penny ¾ in, quarter 1 in (severe), golf ball 1¾ in, baseball 2¾ in. States/Nationals: an IDF curve shows rainfall intensity versus duration for a return period. Longer duration = lower intensity. Rarer storms (100-yr) plot higher. Chance in any year = 1 ÷ return period.
