## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
Voltage is the push (J/C), current is the flow (C/s), resistance is the opposition. Water analogy: pressure = voltage, flow = current, narrow pipe = resistance, pump = battery. Resistance of a wire: R = ρL/A. Longer → more R. Thicker → less R (area goes with diameter squared: double the diameter, R ÷ 4). Hotter metal → more R. ρ: silver 1.59, copper 1.68, gold 2.44, aluminum 2.65 (×10⁻⁸ Ω·m). Glass and rubber ≈ 10¹⁰ to 10¹⁶ Ω·m or more. Ohmic parts have a straight-line I–V graph (slope 1/R). Diodes and LEDs do not. Resistor power rating: a ¼ W, 100 Ω resistor can carry at most √(0.25/100) = 50 mA. Energy: E = P·t. Use kW × hours for kWh and cost; use W × seconds for joules. 60 W × 8 h = 0.48 kWh. Efficiency = output ÷ input. Battery run time. 3.7 V, 2500 mAh → 3.7 × 2.5 = 9.25 Wh. Into a 2.5 W device → 3.7 h. Into a 100 Ω resistor from 9 V with a 500 mAh cell: I = 0.09 A, t = 0.5/0.09 = 5.6 h. Trap Mixing units. mAh is not Ah. Minutes are not seconds. Check every number has its base unit before multiplying.
