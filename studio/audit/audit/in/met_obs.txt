# meteorology-b / obs: Observation tools  (47 questions, 46 families)
## STUDY NOTE (what students are taught)
On the ground: ASOS (automated airport stations), mesonets (dense local stations), buoys (water). Aloft: radiosondes on balloons, aircraft reports. Radar product Shows Reflectivity How much precipitation, and where Base velocity Motion toward or away from the radar Correlation coefficient (CC) How alike the targets are. Rain is high. Debris is low Storm relative motion Winds with the storm's own movement removed. Shows rotation Composite reflectivity Strongest echo at any height Wind profile Winds at each height over the radar Satellite: visible needs daylight. Infrared (IR) works day and night: cold cloud tops are bright, so tall storms stand out. Water vapor shows moisture in the middle and upper air. Velocity: toward the radar is NEGATIVE (green). Away is POSITIVE (red). Think "red = run away".
