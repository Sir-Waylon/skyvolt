Resources

Trusted places to go deeper. These are run by universities, government science agencies and non-profits.

Circuits and electricity

  • Khan Academy: Electrical engineering — Free video lessons and exercises.
  • PhET Circuit Construction Kit (University of Colorado Boulder) — Build and measure circuits in a simulator.
  • HyperPhysics (Georgia State University) — Concise concept maps for electricity.
  • MIT OpenCourseWare — Free university course materials, including circuits.
  • All About Circuits textbook — Free online electronics textbook.

Meteorology

  • NOAA National Weather Service JetStream — An online school for weather from the NWS.
  • UCAR COMET MetEd — Free professional meteorology training modules.
  • UCAR Center for Science Education — Student-friendly atmosphere explainers.
  • NOAA Storm Prediction Center — Real forecasts, outlooks and storm reports.
  • Penn State Department of Meteorology — Free introductory course notes.

Competition

  • Science Olympiad — Official rules and event information.
