    Voltaic · V, I, R, P, E

    A 1.5 A current flows for 2.0 minutes. About how many electrons pass a point? (e = 1.60×10⁻¹⁹ C) Answer in scientific notation, e.g. 1.1e21.

    e+21 electrons
    Answer: 1.125e+21 electrons. 180 ÷ 1.60×10⁻¹⁹ = 1.1×10²¹. Notes|n5|Current, voltage, resistance, power, energy (area iv)

    Stratus · Setup: jets, fronts, shear

    The wind is 15 knots from the south at the surface and 55 knots from the south at 6 km. What is the wind speed shear in knots?

    kt
    Answer: 40 kt. The speed shear is 40 knots. Notes|n-setup|Setup for severe weather

    Voltaic · Control devices

    A circuit breaker's bimetal strip bends and trips the breaker when it is hot. Why does it bend?

    Two bonded metals expand at different rates, so the strip curves when heated.

    Stratus · Precipitation and floods

    In the Bergeron process, ice crystals grow because…

    Vapor pressure over ice is lower, so ice grows at droplets' expense.

    Voltaic · Kirchhoff

    Two sources share a middle branch. E1 = 19 V with R1 = 2 Ω; E2 = 17 V with R2 = 4 Ω; the shared branch has R3 = 6 Ω. The current in R3 is:

    A
    Answer: 2.5 A. I₁ + I₂ = I₃; 19 = 2I₁ + 6I₃; 17 = 4I₂ + 6I₃. Solving gives I₁ = 2.0 A, I₂ = 0.5 A, I₃ = 2.5 A. Notes|n7|Kirchhoff's laws (area ix, States and Nationals)

    Stratus · Severe thunderstorms

    Wind force on a wall scales with wind speed squared. If gusts go from 30 mph to 60 mph, by what factor does force grow?

    ×
    Answer: 4 ×. Small speed rises produce big damage. Notes|n-tstorm|Severe thunderstorms

    Stratus · Hurricanes

    Which condition helps hurricanes form?

    They need heat, moisture and a vertical structure.

    Voltaic · Capacitance

    A 100 pF air capacitor has its plate gap doubled. Find the new capacitance in picofarads.

    pF
    Answer: 50 pF. Plates farther apart feel each other less. Notes|n13|Capacitance (adjacent)

    Stratus · Historic cases and data

    The Tri-State Tornado track was about 219 miles long. At an average speed of 60 mph, how many hours did it take to travel that distance?

    h
    Answer: 3.65 h. 219 ÷ 60 ≈ 3.65 hours. Notes|n-cases|Historic events and case analysis
